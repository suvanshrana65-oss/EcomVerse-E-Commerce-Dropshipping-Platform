SLIDE 02

Introduction

E-commerce dominates modern retail, accounting for 25% of global revenue with 67% of purchases on mobile. However, traditional models demand heavy inventory investment, warehousing, and logistics—creating barriers for startups and SMEs.

The dropshipping model eliminates these barriers: products ship directly from suppliers to customers, removing inventory risk and reducing operational complexity.

EcomVerse is a full-stack MERN platform designed for dropshipping entrepreneurs and students. It delivers automated product management, secure payments (Stripe/Razorpay), ML-driven recommendations, and real-time analytics—all in a scalable, educational framework.

Literature Review

E-Commerce Evolution (2020–2025)

Platforms evolved from simple catalogs to sophisticated solutions. Mobile commerce dominates with 67% of purchases on mobile, driven by faster internet and device proliferation.

AI Personalization

AI-powered recommendations and dynamic pricing deliver 20–30% higher conversion rates. Modern shoppers expect tailored experiences across all touchpoints.

Security & Payments

Payment fraud and cyber threats make robust gateways (Stripe, Razorpay) with advanced authentication essential for trust and compliance.

Dropshipping Model

Eliminates inventory barriers through supplier APIs and real-time sync. Enables rapid experimentation without cash flow challenges—ideal for students and entrepreneurs.

Technology Stack

  • MERN Stack: Unified JavaScript for rapid development
  • Cloud/DevOps: Scalable multi-region deployment
  • ML & Automation: Chatbots to fraud detection
